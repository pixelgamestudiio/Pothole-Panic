# Pothole Panic Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2 October 2026  
**Developer:** Loop Games  
**Contact:** [pixelgamestudiio@gmail.com](mailto:pixelgamestudiio@gmail.com)

This policy describes the Android version of **Pothole Panic**, a driving game by Loop Games. It explains what the app stores, what its advertising and consent software may process, and the choices available to players.

## Information stored on your device

The game stores progress and settings on your device, including high scores, completed runs, coins, unlocked and selected vehicles, sound preferences, and the broad age group selected at first launch. The game asks for an age group, not a date of birth, and stores that choice locally. This information is not sent to a game account or our own game server. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling it removes locally stored game data.

Pothole Panic does not create player accounts, maintain an online leaderboard, request precise location, or access contacts, photos, the microphone, or the camera. Players may choose to share a score or invite code through Android's share sheet; the chosen app then receives the shared text under that app's privacy policy.

If you email our support address, we receive the email address and any information you choose to include. We use it only to answer your request and retain it in our email service for as long as needed to handle that request.

## Advertising and consent services

The Android app includes Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) and Google Mobile Ads SDK. UMP manages required advertising privacy choices. Ads are requested only for players who select the 18-or-older age group. The app does not request or show ads, including rewarded and full-screen ads, for players who select an age under 18. Before SDK initialization, the app applies Google's age-restricted ad treatment and a G-rated ad content limit.

When these Google SDKs operate, Google may automatically process and receive information such as the device's IP address (which can indicate approximate location), advertising or other device identifiers, interactions with the app and ads, and diagnostics about app or SDK performance. Google says this information is encrypted in transit. Google's processing is governed by [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google's information about advertising privacy](https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads). Players can manage advertising identifiers in Android settings and review available ad privacy choices in the app.

The current Android release does not enable Firebase Analytics or RevenueCat purchases. It has no in-game real-money purchases, and the game developer does not receive payment-card details.

## Children and families

Pothole Panic is intended for players of all ages. The first-launch age screen offers the same neutral choices to everyone. The app does not ask children for a name, email address, date of birth, or other direct contact information. It does not show ads or enable analytics or purchase features for players who select an age under 18. The Google advertising and consent SDKs remain subject to Google's own privacy practices as described above.

Parents or guardians with privacy questions can contact us at [pixelgamestudiio@gmail.com](mailto:pixelgamestudiio@gmail.com). If a child has provided information to us directly, please contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action.

## Retention and deletion

Game progress is stored on the player's device until the app's storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. The developer does not keep account profiles or game progress on its own servers. Google handles information processed by its SDKs according to Google's policies and controls linked above; Android's advertising-ID controls are available in device settings.

## Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app or its data practices change. The effective date at the top shows when this policy was last revised. For questions, contact **Loop Games** at [pixelgamestudiio@gmail.com](mailto:pixelgamestudiio@gmail.com).
